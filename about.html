The brand

Who is
Weird Kid?

The ones who never fit in — and never wanted to.

🎨

Est. 2024

We were born
different.

Weird Kid didn't start in a boardroom. It started in a bedroom — with a sketchbook full of characters that didn't belong anywhere, and an idea that those who felt like outsiders deserved clothing that said it out loud.

Our founder grew up between cities, cultures, and cliques — never quite fitting into any single box. That feeling became the foundation of everything we make. Weird Kid is for the skaters, the artists, the dreamers, the misfits, and the ones who look at the world sideways.

Every hoodie, every tee, every piece we drop is a statement. It says: I'm not normal, and I'm proud of it.
